# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages from a public repo (user's choice).

## Users

Wedding guests who receive a link, almost always on a phone, usually from a chat message. They open it once to feel invited, then come back for the practical details (when, where, what to wear) closer to the day.

## Product Purpose

A digital wedding invitation. It opens on a sealed envelope the guest taps to open; the invitation comes out of it, and the rest of the page carries the details a paper suite would carry. Success: guests feel personally invited and can find time, place, and dress code within seconds.

## Positioning

It behaves like the physical stationery it replaces: an envelope that actually opens, a card that actually comes out.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Envelope-first intro is mandatory (user brief, with reference image of a pale blue envelope with a blind-embossed monogram on the flap tip and a "tap to open" prompt).
- All couple-specific facts live in `config.js`.
- RSVP: undecided. The user will choose a method later; the page keeps a slot for it.
- Background music: optional; only shown when an audio file is configured.

## Brand Commitments

- Pale blue envelope, embossed script monogram, "tap to open" (from the user's reference image).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. Names, date, venue, schedule, and FAQ are placeholders (user asked for placeholders). Do not present them as real.
